### English recording script

This whole week I prepared the DalaHold deck and pitch for the Crypto World's Fair and the Superteam Kazakhstan track. I built an 11-slide deck on the Superteam template and went through three rounds of mentor feedback: every amount in dollars, a label on every slide, real cases where farmers handed over animals and were never paid, a competitor table and a revenue calculation. I ran three independent ProofPilot reviews: we removed overstated claims and checked every number against its source.

Two farms, Mambetov and Co. and Namys, and a buyer of breeding bulls agreed verbally to pilot. I switched the demo to a livestock deal and made the repository public. I also prepared the talk, answers to likely jury questions and the submission checklist.

Product code barely changed this week: a deal signed by two people and the first devnet transaction are still ahead. That is the next step.

## Proposed focus for 12–18 October / Предлагаемый фокус на 12–18 октября

These are priorities, not a dated commitment. / Это приоритеты, а не календарное обязательство.

1. **Submission / Подача.** By 12 October 23:59 PT: the product demo video (≤ 3 min), the pitch video (≤ 2 min), profiles and the repository link; merge the demo branch into `main` and choose a license. / До 12 октября 23:59 PT: демо-видео продукта (≤ 3 мин), pitch-видео (≤ 2 мин), профили и ссылка на репозиторий; влить ветку демо в `main` и выбрать лицензию.

3. **First pilot sessions / Первые пилотные сессии.** Replay a recent deal with Mambetov and Co., Namys and the buyer; continue only if at least 3 of 4 pairs finish within 10 minutes and 2 agree to a second test. / Воспроизвести недавнюю сделку с «Мамбетов и К», «Намыс» и покупателем; продолжать, только если минимум 3 из 4 пар укладываются в 10 минут и двое соглашаются на повторный тест.
